    How to use
    1. 1

      Tap on ✎ and add your initials.

    2. 2

      Tap + 📷 to add your first camera, then pick its brand and model.

    3. 3

      Check your settings: set each one on the camera, then tick it.

    4. 4

      Add additional cameras if needed with + Add camera.

    5. 5

      Tap on 📷→🕒 when done.

    6. 6

      Shoot the clock with every camera.

    7. 7

      Play back the clock shot on the camera, tap 📱→📷 and photograph the camera's screen.

    8. 8

      If the times match to the second, tap ✓ Yes, in sync. If not, fix the camera's clock and tap 📷 Retake.

    9. 9

      Tap Next camera → for the next camera's screen.

    10. 10

      Tap 🖼️→📨, then 📨 and pick your group chat.

    11. ⤓
      Install the app

      · iPhone (Safari): tap Share □↑ (on newer iOS it's under ⋯), then Add to Home Screen → Add.
      · Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ top right, then Add to home screen → Install.

    12. i
      Good to know

      · Open the box with ⤢ to switch, edit, delete or add cameras.
      · Tap the camera name in the white box to change it to Hand cam or LS.
      · Sync now: re-checks the time server.
      · Tap the clock: hides / shows the buttons.
      · Rotate button (top right): turns the app sideways.
      · Ticks and photos reset 12 hours after sending.
      · Reset app (tap twice): clears everything.
